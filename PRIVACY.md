# Life Minder Privacy

Life Minder is a local-first application. Core features do not require an account, internet connection, remote server, analytics service, advertising service, or API key. The Android manifest does not request internet access.

Projects, tasks, timers, alarms, work sessions, settings, and reviews remain on the device unless the user explicitly exports or backs them up. Exports and backups are written to Life Minder's application documents directory and remain under the user's control.

Life Minder does not transmit personal data. Uninstalling the application may remove app-private data unless Android backup or a user-created Life Minder export/backup preserves it.
